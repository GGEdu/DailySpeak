City council approves sweeping overhaul of public transport

The city council has approved a sweeping overhaul of its public transport network, promising faster buses and cheaper fares within three years.

Mayor José Núñez said the reform was "long overdue" and would reduce congestion in the city centre, where traffic has worsened steadily over the past decade.

Opposition councillors argued that the plan was overly ambitious and warned that the budget did not account for rising construction costs or the need to retrain drivers.

Transport campaigners broadly welcomed the vote, but cautioned that previous attempts at reform had stalled because of disagreements between neighbouring authorities.
